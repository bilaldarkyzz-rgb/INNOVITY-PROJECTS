# INNOVITY-PROJECTS

## AI-Based Smart Traffic Light System for Emergency Ambulances

A smart city solution designed to reduce emergency response delays by integrating artificial intelligence, IoT, GPS, and real-time traffic monitoring to create a safe and efficient emergency corridor for ambulances.

## Overview

Emergency medical response depends heavily on time. In critical situations such as heart attacks, strokes, severe accidents, and respiratory emergencies, even a few minutes of delay can significantly reduce a patient's chances of survival. Traditional traffic systems are often not designed to prioritize emergency vehicles, leading to congestion at intersections, delayed response times, and increased risk to patients.

INNOVITY-PROJECTS addresses this challenge by developing an advanced AI-powered smart traffic light system that enables ambulances to move through traffic more efficiently and safely. The system connects ambulances, traffic signals, hospitals, and a central traffic management platform to create a coordinated emergency route in real time.

## Problem Statement

Ambulance delays are commonly caused by:

- Heavy traffic congestion
- Poorly coordinated traffic signal timings
- Road blockages and lane obstructions
- Limited emergency priority routing at intersections
- Lack of real-time communication between emergency vehicles and traffic infrastructure

In emergency situations, these delays can affect patient survival and recovery outcomes. A smarter traffic management approach is essential to improve emergency response efficiency and protect public health.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed system uses Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), GPS navigation, and real-time traffic data analysis to manage emergency vehicle movement more effectively.

When an ambulance receives an emergency call, its GPS location and destination hospital are transmitted to the AI-based traffic control system. The system analyzes:

- Real-time traffic density
- Road conditions
- Vehicle movement patterns
- Signal timing data
- Potential obstacles and congestion points

Using this information, it calculates the fastest practical route and prepares a coordinated emergency corridor along the ambulance's route.

## How the System Works

### 1. Emergency Dispatch and Route Planning
The system receives the ambulance's current position and destination hospital, then evaluates the route using live traffic information to identify the most efficient path.

### 2. Traffic Signal Priority
As the ambulance approaches an intersection, the system communicates with the relevant smart traffic signal and temporarily gives the ambulance's direction priority by switching the signal to green while safely controlling conflicting traffic.

### 3. Multi-Intersection Coordination
Instead of changing only one signal, the AI coordinates multiple traffic lights along the ambulance's route. This creates a continuous emergency corridor that minimizes stopping and reduces congestion buildup at follow-up intersections.

### 4. Real-Time Traffic Monitoring
Computer vision cameras, roadside sensors, and connected devices help detect:

- Traffic density
- Blocked lanes or road obstructions
- Vehicles that are not clearing the ambulance path

This allows the system to adapt and reroute when needed.

### 5. Driver Awareness and Safety Alerts
Nearby drivers can be notified through navigation applications, connected vehicle systems, and roadside warning displays, encouraging them to move aside safely without causing panic or unsafe driving behavior.

### 6. Hospital Preparedness
Hospitals receive the ambulance's estimated arrival time and emergency information, allowing them to prepare equipment, emergency beds, and specialist staff before the patient arrives.
